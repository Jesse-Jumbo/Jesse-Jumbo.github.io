06 / Game development · Templates · Team leadership2022–2023

PTWA Special-Education Games

Around 27 games, shared templates, and teamwork

Developed around 27 special-education games and reusable templates, and led two NCKU students in collaborative development, turning teaching needs into approachable interactions.

  • JavaScript
  • HTML
  • CSS
  • Canvas
Project details

The context

Lessons cover numbers, fractions, time, volume, and weight. Controls, prompts, and feedback need to suit learner abilities, while the development approach must support more games.

Design & implementation

  • Implemented matching, drag-and-drop, and selection interactions with JavaScript, HTML, CSS, and Canvas.
  • Translated number-and-hand-gesture matching and Grade 3 math requirements into common interaction patterns and game templates.
  • Used AI tools to help identify templates and implement code, then validated and revised the games against their requirements.
  • Led two NCKU students in collaborative development using the teaching requirements and shared templates.

What matters here

Reusable templates help a team extend more lesson ideas, while each game still needs to fit how learners interact and understand.
